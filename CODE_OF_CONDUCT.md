# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in this project — issues, pull requests, discussions, and any other interaction — to be harassment-free for everyone, regardless of experience, background, identity, or anything else unrelated to the quality of a contribution.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive in issues, pull requests, and discussions.
- Assume good faith, and give clear, actionable feedback.
- Accept disagreement gracefully; not every suggestion will be adopted.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks, in public or private communication related to this project.
- Publishing others' private information without consent.
- Sustained disruption of issues, pull requests, or discussions.
- Impersonating WiselyWise, Smart Maya, or this project's maintainers.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, issues, and pull requests that violate this Code of Conduct, and may temporarily or permanently block a contributor for behavior they deem inappropriate, threatening, or harmful.

Report unacceptable behavior to **security@wiselywise.com**. Reports are handled discreetly, and we will acknowledge receipt within 5 business days.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within this repository's spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions, commits) and in any other setting when an individual is representing this project or its community.
